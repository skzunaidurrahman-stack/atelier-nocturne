Chapter 03 — Materials

Nothing here reflects a photograph. Everything reflects a room.

Carrara

A

Veined by a river that ran 200 million years before the name. Clearcoated only where it meets the hand.

Bronze

B

Copper and tin, poured at 1 100 °C. Brushed, never polished — patina needs a place to sit.

Silk velvet

C

Woven with a sheen face so a single lamp travels the entire drape as you walk past it.
